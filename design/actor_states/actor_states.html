Actor State Machine

Visual representation of the 4-state actor movement system

Legend & Key Concepts

🟠 Orange
Collision-enabled states: Actor subject to normal collision physics (NO_SUBCELL, IDLE)
🔵 Blue
Collision-immune states: Actor phases through others (PSC_ALIGNMENT, MOVE)
🟢 1°
Primary Subcell Reservation: Persistent reservation defining actor's "home" subcell. Released only when changing to new primary subcell.
🟣 2+
Additional Reservations: Temporary diagonal + anchor subcells reserved during MOVE state. Released when target is reached.
🔒 Locked
During MOVE state, values are locked in actor structure: locked_target_position, locked_affinity, locked_anchor_subcell
🔄 Infinite
Both NO_SUBCELL and IDLE states retry indefinitely when blocked (no timeout mechanism)
📐 Rectangle
"Rectangle boundary" targets refer to ray-rectangle intersection calculated per actor_directing_v2.txt Section B
Design Principle: Affinity and target position are determined (locked) once diagonal is reserved, based on actor's precise float position. Values remain constant throughout MOVE state.
